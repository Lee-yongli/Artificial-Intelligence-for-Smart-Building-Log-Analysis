Analysis Workflow

Current pipeline
iWhy public data? Smart Facilities SA operational logs were not available during development. CU-BEMS was selected as a structurally relevant smart-building dataset for developing and evaluating a transferable workflow; its learned thresholds do not describe the client's buildings.
1

Map and validate

Connect client BMS exports, map timestamps, zones, equipment variables and units, then report missing or invalid fields.

2

Rebuild local baselines

Learn normal operating patterns for each client building and period; do not reuse CU-BEMS thresholds as production settings.

3

Verify with operators

Run a monitored pilot, collect facilities-team feedback and calibrate alert volume before operational use.
